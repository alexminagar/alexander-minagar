Behind the interface

Make room
for the question.

The interface keeps a single card in focus. Answers stay hidden until you ask for them, and the deck position makes it easy to see where you are.

Every deck starts in a fixed order. At the end, you can repeat it, shuffle it, switch subjects, or explore another project. There are no accounts or saved study records.
